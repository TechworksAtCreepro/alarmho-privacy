AlarmHo privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25.

Who we are. AlarmHo is published by Zelerius Tech Pvt Ltd, 4/293, Arihant Technopolis, Rajiv Gandhi Salai, Perungudi, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600096, India. Contact: support@zelerius.com.

What the app does. AlarmHo connects your phone to small Bluetooth trackers you own and warns you when one moves out of range or loses its connection.

Data the app stores on your phone. The Bluetooth address and the name, icon, colour and alert settings you give each tracker; a history of alerts (when a tracker went out of range, lost its link, or was turned off); your app settings. If you turn on "Remember last seen location", the phone's position at the moment a tracker went out of range or lost its link is stored with that tracker.

Data that leaves your phone. None. AlarmHo has no server, no account and no analytics. Data stays on the device and in your own Android backup (Google backup or device-to-device transfer) if you have backup enabled.

Permissions. Nearby devices (Bluetooth) to find and talk to trackers; the app never uses Bluetooth to work out your location. Notifications to alert you. Location only if you opt in to "Remember last seen location", and only at the moment an alert happens. Run in the background (foreground service with a persistent notification) so the link to your trackers stays alive while the app is not on screen.

Children. AlarmHo can be attached to a child's bag or clothing. The app does not collect personal information about the child; the tracker only reports its distance from the parent's phone.

Deleting data. Forget a tracker from its screen, clear the alert history from the Alerts tab, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all data (except what your Android backup already holds, which you control in Android settings).

Changes. We will update this page and the "last updated" date when the policy changes.

Crash reports

If AlarmHo closes unexpectedly, it saves a short technical report on your device (app version, Android version, device model and the error details). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose Share report, in which case you pick the app it goes to. You can delete the report from Settings at any time.
